## Rules (must follow)

- **Never** use images from Google Images, Pinterest, Dribbble, or competitor websites
- Download only from: Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, Wikimedia Commons
- Before use, open the image's page and confirm the licence permits **commercial use**
- Record the exact page URL and photographer name in this file
- Do **not** present stock photos as the owner's real jobs — captions must not imply otherwise
- Strip EXIF data before publishing (removes hidden GPS coordinates)
- Convert to WebP, compress: hero under 250 KB, cards under 100 KB, gallery under 80 KB
